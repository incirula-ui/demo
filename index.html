Frequently Asked Questions

How long must I wait for the permanent restoration?

After implant placement, osseointegration (fusion of the implant to the jawbone) takes approximately 8-12 weeks. Proper healing is essential before load bearing.

Is the dental implant surgery painful?

Dental implant surgeries are generally painless procedures performed under local anesthesia. Sedation and general anesthesia are also options depending on the patient's comfort level.
