Frequently asked questions

How long until the permanent restoration? +

Osseointegration — fusion of implant to jawbone — takes approximately 8–12 weeks. Proper healing is essential before load-bearing.

Is dental implant surgery painful? +

It's generally a painless procedure under local anesthesia. Sedation and general anesthesia are also available depending on patient comfort.

What materials are PTI implants made from? +

Grade 5 titanium alloy (Ti-6Al-4V), the clinical standard for strength, biocompatibility, and long-term durability.

Do you support clinics outside Turkey? +

Yes — PTI International ships to distributors and clinics in more than 40 countries with full documentation and support.

What is the warranty period? +

All PTI implants include a 10-year manufacturer's warranty and comprehensive after-sales technical support with documentation traceability.
